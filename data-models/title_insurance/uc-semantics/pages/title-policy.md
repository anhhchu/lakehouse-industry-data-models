# Title Policy

A title policy is an issued contract of title insurance covering an identified insured estate or interest subject to its terms, conditions, exceptions, and endorsements.

## Business Rules

- Every policy references its originating title order.
- Owner and lender policies are separate records and policy types.
- A policy is distinct from its commitment.
- Corrections, endorsements, cancellations, and voids preserve prior versions.
- Record insured parties, covered property, estate or interest, amount, effective date, exceptions, issuing agent, and underwriter.
