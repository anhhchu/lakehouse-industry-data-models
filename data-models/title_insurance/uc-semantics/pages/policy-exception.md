# Policy Exception

A policy exception is a matter excluded from proposed or issued title-insurance coverage. An exception can originate from a title finding, standard form language, underwriting decision, or jurisdictional requirement.

## Business Rules

- An exception is distinct from a title requirement.
- Commitment exceptions and final policy exceptions must remain versioned and traceable.
- Record whether an exception is standard, specific, removed, modified, or carried into the final policy.
- Preserve the evidence or authority supporting a specific exception.

## Common Uses

- Compare proposed and final coverage.
- Identify recurring specific exceptions by jurisdiction or defect category.
- Evaluate claims against the policy's effective exceptions.
