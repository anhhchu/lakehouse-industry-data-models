# Title Order

A title order is a request for title, settlement, recording, or related services for a real-estate transaction. It is the coordinating business record for requested products, property, parcels, participants, service channel, due dates, assignments, and operational milestones.

## Business Rules

- One order can concern multiple properties or parcels.
- An order can request multiple services and produce multiple commitments or policies.
- Material status changes are retained as history rather than overwritten.
- Amendments, cancellations, reopenings, transfers, and splits retain their relationship to the originating order.
- A title order is not a title commitment or title policy.

## Common Uses

- Measure order volume and cycle time.
- Identify work approaching a contractual due date.
- Relate title, settlement, recording, policy, and claim activity to the originating request.
