# Title Examination

Title examination is the professional evaluation of title evidence and apparent title condition. It interprets search results and produces evidence-backed findings, requirements, proposed exceptions, or underwriting referrals.

## Business Rules

- Examination is distinct from evidence collection or title search.
- Every material finding cites supporting evidence.
- Preserve examiner, examination version, completion timestamp, and review history.
- Classification changes remain historically available.
- Matters beyond delegated authority create an underwriting referral rather than an implicit approval.
