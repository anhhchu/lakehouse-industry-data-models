# Curative Action

A curative action is an activity intended to resolve, mitigate, or document a title finding or requirement. Examples include obtaining a release, corrective instrument, affidavit, authority document, payoff confirmation, or underwriting approval.

## Business Rules

- One action can address multiple findings, and one finding can require multiple actions.
- Record owner, due date, action type, status, outcome, and supporting evidence.
- Completion of an action does not automatically mean its requirement is satisfied.
- Preserve unsuccessful and superseded actions for auditability.
