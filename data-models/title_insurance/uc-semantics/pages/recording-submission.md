# Recording Submission

A recording submission is an attempt to submit a document or recording package to a recording jurisdiction. Multiple submissions can occur before final acceptance.

## Business Rules

- Preserve each submission, response, rejection reason, correction, and resubmission.
- Package status and individual document status are separate.
- Submission, acceptance, and official recording timestamps are distinct.
- Instrument number or book and page belongs to recording confirmation, not the initial submission.
