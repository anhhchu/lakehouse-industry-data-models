# Policy, Agency, and Claims Guide

## Policies

Owner and lender policies are distinct policy products. Each policy references its originating title order, covered property or parcels, insured parties, insured estate or interest, policy amount, effective date, exceptions, endorsements, issuing agent, and underwriter. A policy can be corrected, endorsed, cancelled, or voided without overwriting prior versions.

## Endorsements

An endorsement modifies or clarifies coverage and must reference the affected policy. Record the endorsement form, jurisdiction, effective date, premium impact, requested party, approval, and issuance status.

## Agency Reporting and Remittance

Independent agents report issued policies and related premium. The company compares reported policy activity, expected premium, received remittance, retained commission, taxes, and fees. Differences become remittance exceptions with ownership, aging, and resolution.

## Claims

A claim references a policy and claimant and can reference title findings, evidence, recorded instruments, underwriting decisions, and prior curative work. Record alleged defect, coverage position, investigation, litigation, reserves, expenses, indemnity payments, recoveries, subrogation, disposition, and closure.

## Financial Distinctions

- Alleged loss is not necessarily covered loss.
- Defense expense is distinct from indemnity.
- Case reserve is distinct from paid amount.
- Recovery and subrogation reduce net loss but remain separate transactions.
- Policy premium and claim financial activity use different accounting lifecycles.

## Demonstration Edge Cases

- A claim is asserted against both an owner policy and a lender policy.
- Coverage is partially accepted with one allegation excluded.
- A claim reopens after closure.
- A payment is recovered through subrogation.
- An agent reports a policy in a later remittance period than its issue date.
